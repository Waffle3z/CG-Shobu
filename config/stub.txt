gameloop
read round:int myColor:int
loop 8 read row:word(8)
read lastMove:string(32)
read moveCount:int
loop moveCount read move:string(32)
write 3 7 4 3 0 -1

INPUT
round: 0 or 1 (in round 1 each player starts with the color the other player started with in round 0)
myColor: the color you play this turn: 0 = black (home boards at the bottom, y >= 4), 1 = white (home boards at the top); it changes after a swap
row: one row of the 8x8 grid, top to bottom: . empty, b black stone, w white stone
lastMove: your opponent's last move as "px py ax ay dx dy", SWAP if your opponent just swapped, or - at the start of a round
moveCount: number of legal moves
move: a legal move "px py ax ay dx dy"; on white's first turn of a round the last one is SWAP (pie rule)

OUTPUT
Print one of the legal moves listed in the input (for example the first one). The default below is only legal as black's first move.
px py ax ay dx dy: move your stone at (px,py) on one of your home boards by (dx,dy), then your stone at (ax,ay) on a board of the other color by the same (dx,dy); or SWAP on white's first turn of a round; optionally followed by a message (40 characters shown)
