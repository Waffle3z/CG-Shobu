gameloop
read round:int myColor:int
loop 8 read row:word(8)
read lastAction:string(32)
read actionCount:int
loop actionCount read action:string(32)
write 3 7 4 3 0 -1

INPUT
round: 1 or 2 (in round 2 each player starts with the color the other player started with in round 1)
myColor: the color you play this turn: 0 = black, 1 = white; it changes after a swap
row: one row of the 8x8 grid, top to bottom: . empty, b black stone, w white stone
lastAction: your opponent's last action as "px py ax ay dx dy", SWAP if your opponent just swapped, or - at the start of a round
actionCount: number of legal actions
action: a legal action "px py ax ay dx dy"; on white's first turn of a round the last one is SWAP (pie rule)

OUTPUT
Print one of the legal actions listed in the input (for example the first one). The default below is only legal as black's first action.
px py ax ay dx dy: move your stone at (px,py) on one of your home boards by (dx,dy), then your stone at (ax,ay) on a board of the other color by the same (dx,dy); or SWAP on white's first turn of a round; optionally followed by a message (40 characters shown)
